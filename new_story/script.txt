In 1904, the Olympic marathon winner had ridden eleven miles in a car.

See, it was ninety degrees in St. Louis, and Fred Lorz quit at mile nine.

A passing car picked him up and drove him eleven miles.

The car broke down, so Fred jogged the rest.

He crossed the line first, and Alice Roosevelt crowned him with a wreath.

Someone yelled, "That's him in the car!"

Fred said it was a joke.

The judges banned him for life.

A year later, he won Boston.

No car this time.
